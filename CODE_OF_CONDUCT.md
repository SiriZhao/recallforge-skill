# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community for students, educators, and contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, respectful, and considerate.
- Respect privacy, copyright, and academic integrity.
- Give feedback about ideas and implementations, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or abusive language.
- Publishing secrets, personal data, restricted exams, or unauthorized course materials.
- Encouraging cheating or violations of institutional rules.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close issues, or limit participation to keep the community safe.
